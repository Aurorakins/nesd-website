About us

NESD has been serving the medical community in the northeast region since 1998 with our home office located in Wallingford, Connecticut. Each of our certified couriers receive 5 days of hands-on training to ensure that your patients, their families, and the medical providers are receiving the best possible service and care. Each specimen is handled logistically. We scan each batch sheet and pack the specimens according to temperature. Every specimen is packed with care and delivered on time.
