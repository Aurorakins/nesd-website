Since 1998, New England Same Day has moved specimens between hospitals, physician offices,
laboratories, and research programs across the Northeast. Our certified couriers know that every
sample represents a patient waiting on a result, and they handle each one that way.

### What we transport

* Blood, urine, and other clinical specimens
* Pathology and tissue samples
* Research and clinical trial samples
* Specimens that must stay at room temperature, refrigerated, or frozen

### How we handle every pickup

* **Checked against the paperwork.** We scan each batch sheet so nothing leaves a site unaccounted for.
* **Packed by temperature.** Every specimen is packed according to its temperature requirements and kept that way until delivery.
* **Delivered on time.** Scheduled daily routes for recurring pickups, and STAT runs when a result can't wait.

### Routes that fit your schedule

Set up a recurring route between your collection sites and your lab, or call us for a one-time pickup.
We run established routes in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New York, New Jersey, and Rhode Island, and we're
available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
