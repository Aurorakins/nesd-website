When a medical delivery can't wait, New England Same Day gets it there. Our certified couriers are
available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for urgent and scheduled deliveries between hospitals,
laboratories, pharmacies, blood banks, and other healthcare facilities across the Northeast.

### What we deliver

* **Pharmaceuticals.** Medications moved between pharmacies, hospitals, and care facilities, on recurring routes or on demand.
* **Blood products.** Blood and blood components transported between blood banks and hospitals.
* **Other time-critical medical items.** If it's medical and it's urgent, call us to talk through what you need moved.

### Why organizations choose NESD

* **Available around the clock.** Nights, weekends, and holidays included.
* **Handled at the right temperature.** Every shipment is packed according to its temperature requirements.
* **Trained couriers.** Every courier completes hands-on training before running a route.
* **Regional reach.** Established routes in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New York, New Jersey, and Rhode Island.
* **Competitive pricing.** Dependable, around-the-clock service at rates that fit your budget, with pricing for both one-time deliveries and recurring routes.
